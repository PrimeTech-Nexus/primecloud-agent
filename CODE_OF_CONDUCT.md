# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

PrimeCloud is committed to a professional, respectful, and inclusive
working environment for everyone contributing to the platform.

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Professional and respectful communication.
- Constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the platform.
- Respect for differing viewpoints.
- Accountability for mistakes.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting comments, or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Any conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all PrimeCloud project spaces,
and also when an individual is representing the project or its
community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project
maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly
and fairly. Project maintainers will respect the privacy and security
of the reporter of any incident.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers will follow these guidelines when determining
consequences for any action they deem in violation of this Code of
Conduct:

1. **Correction** — A private warning.
2. **Warning** — A warning with consequences for continued behavior.
3. **Temporary ban** — A temporary ban from any interaction.
4. **Permanent ban** — A permanent ban from the project.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant
